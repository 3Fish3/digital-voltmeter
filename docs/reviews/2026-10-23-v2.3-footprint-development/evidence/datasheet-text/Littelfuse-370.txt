                                                             Description
                                                             The 370 Series are sub-miniature TR5® fuses, fast acting type,
                                                             250V rated fuses, designed in accordance to IEC 60127-3.



                                                             Features & Benefits
                                                             ■ Reduced PCB space                  ■ Available from 0.040A
                                                               requirements                         to 6.3A
                                                             ■ Direct solderable or               ■ UL Recognized to UL/CSA/
                                                               plug-in versions                     NMX 248-1 and UL/CSA/NMX
                                                             ■ Internationally approved             248-14
                                                             ■ Low internal resistance            ■ Conforms to EN/J 60127-1
                                                                                                    and EN/J 60127-3
                                                             ■ Shock safe casing
                                                                                                  ■ Conforms to GB/T9364.1 and
                                                             ■ Vibration resistant                  GB/T9364.3
                                                             ■ Lead-free, Halogen free and
Additional Information                                         RoHS compliant



                                                             Applications
                                                             ■ Battery Chargers                   ■ Power supplies
                                                             ■ Consumer Electronics               ■ Industrial Controllers
